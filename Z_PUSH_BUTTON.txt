*TO CREATE IMPLICIT WORK AREA
TABLES SSCRFIELDS.
PARAMETERS N1 TYPE I.
SELECTION-SCREEN SKIP 2.
*SYNTAX
*SELECTION-SCREEN PUSHBUTTON /<POS>(<LEN>) <PUSH> USER-COMMAND <FCODE>.
SELECTION-SCREEN PUSHBUTTON /10(10) PUSH1 USER-COMMAND FCT1.
SELECTION-SCREEN PUSHBUTTON 40(10) PUSH2 USER-COMMAND FCT2.
INITIALIZATION.
 PUSH1 = 'DISPLAY'.
 PUSH2 = 'EXIT'.
AT SELECTION-SCREEN.
 IF SSCRFIELDS-UCOMM = 'FCT2'.
 LEAVE PROGRAM.
 ENDIF.
